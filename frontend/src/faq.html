Our website uses a selection of indexes carefully chosen to grade environmental impact, healthiness of the product and processing.

Nutriscore

The Nutri-Score is a french 5 color nutrition label, now used around the world. It assigns a letter from A (best, color green) to E (worst, color red), based on other foods in the same category.

High content of fruits, vegetables, fibers and protein promotes a preferable score, while a high content of energy, sugar, fat cause a detrimental score.

NOVA

The NOVA classification is a brazilian food ranking system based on food processing applied to the products. It ranks between 1 and 4.

1 being the unprocessed or minimally processed foods, 2 the processed culinary ingredients, 3 the processed foods and 4 the ultra-processed foods.

Eco-Score (or Green-Score)

The Eco-Score has similar grading to the Nutri-Score, ranking products from A to E based on ecological impact. The aim is to help consumers make more ecological choices when making their purchases.

It is calculated by analysing the water use, land use, packaging, country of origin and more.
